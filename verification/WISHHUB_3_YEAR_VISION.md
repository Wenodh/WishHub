# WishHub 3-Year Vision

## 1. Executive Vision
In three years, WishHub will evolve from a personal wishlist tool into a **global e-commerce curation and discovery network**. By providing a universal, platform-agnostic space to save, organize, and track products, WishHub will become the go-to shopping assistant for millions of consumers worldwide.

---


---

## 3. Product Ecosystem Expansion
- **Universal Checkout**: Enable users to buy saved items from multiple merchants in a single transaction directly through WishHub, earning referral commissions on every purchase.
- **Unified Registry Network**: Partner with wedding, baby, and event registries to allow creators to pull products from any online store into a single, cohesive, and easy-to-use registry.
- **Social Discovery Feed**: Build an organic product discovery feed where curators can share public, beautifully formatted shopping collections, driving user growth and engagement.

---

## 4. Engineering & Infrastructure at Scale
- **Serverless and Edge Architecture**: Deploy Next.js and API endpoints on serverless edge networks to minimize latency and ensure fast page loads for global users.
- **Distributed Real-time Scrapers**: Scale the scraping pipeline into an independent service that utilizes distributed queues, rotated proxies, and machine learning to bypass bot-detection systems and parse pages with high accuracy.
- **Vector Search & Personalization**: Power search and recommendations using high-density vector databases (like Pinecone) and image embeddings, helping users find visually similar alternatives or complementary products in real time.
